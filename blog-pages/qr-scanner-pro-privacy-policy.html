QR Scanner Pro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

QR Scanner Pro ("the app") is developed by Engr. Nisar Ahmed Katyar. Your privacy matters — this policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Data stored on your device

  • Scan history: QR codes and barcodes you scan, and QR codes you generate, are stored locally on your device so you can view, search, and favorite them. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.
  • App settings: your theme choice (light/dark), vibration and sound preferences are stored locally.

Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no analytics that identify you.

Permissions used

  • Camera: used only to scan QR codes and barcodes in real time. Camera images are processed on-device and are never uploaded.
  • Photos / media: used only when you choose to scan a QR code from a gallery image.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback when a scan succeeds.

Advertising

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers to show personalized or non-personalized ads according to Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new revision date.
